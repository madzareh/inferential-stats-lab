# inferential-stats-lab

I completed an **Inferential Statistics** course from the University of Amsterdam (UvA) and wanted to apply what I learned to real data.

## Data

The Student Performance data from the UCI repository includes information on 649 students from two schools in Portugal.

## Questions and answers

Q1. Do students who want higher education get better scores?

* Yes. They score about 3.5 points higher on average. The difference is significant. (t-test, d ≈ 1.15)
